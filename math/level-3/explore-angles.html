Explore the eight angles

›

Two parallel lines, r and s, are cut by a transversal t. Eight angles are formed: four at each intersection. Choose an angle to see which angles are equal to it and which ones add up to 180° with it. Move the slider to tilt the transversal: the relationships do not change.
