Making a histogram

›

A histogram groups the data in intervals of the same width and draws one bar per interval, whose height is the frequency: how many values fall in it. The bars touch, because the intervals are continuous. In a bar graph, on the other hand, each bar is a category and they are separated.

Count and draw

Count how many values fall in each interval and type the frequency. The histogram is drawn with your numbers. Each interval includes its left end but not its right end: 20–30 includes 20 but not 30.

Histogram versus box plot

›

The two graphs describe the same data from different angles. The histogram shows the shape and the peaks; the box plot shows the quartiles, the middle spread, and the outliers.

Choosing the most useful display

›

There is no “best” graph in general: it depends on the question.

Which graph answers each kind of question better
If the question is about…Use
the shape, the peaks, or the most frequent intervalthe histogram
the quartiles, the middle spread, or the outliers, or comparing groupsthe box plot
separate categories (countries, colors, days of the week)the bar graph
