How it works

Take it in one sitting, with no interruptions and without looking at the lessons: that way you will really know where you stand before Level 4. Answer the 25 questions, then press Check my answers. You will see why each answer is right and a report by topic with the lessons to review. You pass with 18 right answers (70 %). You may use the calculator and the GED formula sheet, as on the real test.
