Multiplying in three steps

›

To multiply two numbers in scientific notation, the coefficients and the powers of 10 are worked separately and then put together:

1Multiply the coefficients.
2Add the exponents of the powers of 10 (the product rule).
3Check that the coefficient is from 1 to 10, and adjust it if needed.

Three-step calculator

Type two numbers in scientific notation, or ask for an example, and reveal the steps one at a time. Try to predict each step before you show it.

Adjusting the coefficient

›

When you multiply or divide, the coefficient can end up 10 or more (like 20 × 105) or less than 1 (like 0.5 × 10−3). The value is right, but it is not yet valid scientific notation. To fix it, move the point of the coefficient and make up for it with the exponent.

How the exponent makes up for adjusting the coefficient
If the coefficient…The point movesThe exponent
is 10 or moreto the leftgoes up 1 for each place
is less than 1to the rightgoes down 1 for each place

The adjuster

Move the point of the coefficient and watch the exponent make up for each move.

What one loses, the other gains: if the coefficient becomes 10 times smaller, the power of 10 becomes 10 times larger. That is why the value does not change when you adjust.

Practice: write it in valid scientific notation

× 10

Use a decimal point (4.5). For a negative exponent, type the minus sign: −3 or -3.

3

Dividing in scientific notation

›

Division follows the same logic with the opposite operations:

1Divide the coefficients.
2Subtract the exponents: the one on top minus the one on the bottom (the quotient rule).
3Check and adjust the coefficient. In division it often ends up less than 1.

Three-step calculator for dividing

The first number is the dividend (on top) and the second is the divisor (on the bottom).

A common mistake: leaving the answer as 0.5 × 10−3. The arithmetic is right, but it is not valid scientific notation until the coefficient is from 1 to 10: 5 × 10−4.

4

A real calculation

›

In a word problem, decide the operation first. A useful hint:

  • Multiply when an amount repeats many times: speed times time, amount per day times number of days.
  • Divide when a total is shared out, or the question asks “how many fit” or “how much for each one.”

Then use the three steps and write the answer in valid scientific notation, with its unit.

Practice: science and technology problems

× 10

Use a decimal point (4.5). For a negative exponent, type the minus sign: −3 or -3.

On the test: solve it by hand first and use the calculator to confirm. On the TI-30XS, scientific notation is typed with the ×10n key: 3 × 105 is typed as 3, that key, and 5.
