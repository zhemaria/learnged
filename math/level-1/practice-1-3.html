Keywords

›

We add when amounts come together or something increases. We subtract when something is taken away, when we look for what is left, or when we compare two amounts to find the difference.

Words that point to adding or subtracting
➕ Point to adding➖ Point to subtracting
in all, total, sumleft, left over, remain
together, combineddifference, how much more
increased, added, more arriveddecreased, took away
earned, receivedspent, lost, sold
"How many now?" (after more came in)"How much less?" / "How many fewer?"
Keywords are a guide, not a rule. The safest thing is to picture the situation: are the amounts coming together, or is something taken away?

4

Problems with two operations

›

Some problems need two operations, one after the other. Solve them in parts, in order: first what happened first.

Example: a repair shop had 350 parts, received 180, and used 215. First 350 + 180 = 530; then 530 − 215 = 315 parts.
