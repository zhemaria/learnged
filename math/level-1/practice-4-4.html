Mixed numbers and improper fractions

›

A mixed number is a whole number with a fraction: 2 1/2 is "two and one half." The secret of the whole lesson is to change mixed numbers to improper fractions. Mixed to improper: whole number × denominator + numerator, over the same denominator. Improper to mixed: divide; the quotient is the whole number and the remainder is the numerator.

Change it and see the bars

Practice: change it

For a mixed number, type the whole number, a space, and the fraction: 3 1/4. For an improper fraction, use a slash: 13/4.

2

Multiply and divide mixed numbers

›

The rule has no exceptions: never multiply or divide the whole numbers on one side and the fractions on the other. Change everything to improper fractions, then multiply straight across, or keep, change, flip, as in Lesson 4.3.

Example: 2 1/2 × 1 1/3 = 5/2 × 4/3 = 20/6 = 10/3 = 3 1/3.

Practice: multiply or divide

Type a mixed number with a space (2 1/3), a fraction with a slash (7/3), or a whole number. Simplify the fraction part.

3

Add and subtract mixed numbers

›

The lesson's way: change to improper fractions, find a common denominator, add or subtract the numerators, and change back to a mixed number. Another way is to work wholes with wholes and fractions with fractions; when you subtract and the top fraction is too small, you borrow one whole (1 = 4/4, 1 = 8/8). The explorer shows both ways so you can compare them.

Step by step, two ways

Practice: add and subtract

Type a mixed number with a space (2 1/3), a fraction with a slash (7/3), or a whole number. Simplify the fraction part.

4

Mixed numbers in real life

›

Carpentry, recipes, and time: a board 5 1/2 feet long, 1 3/4 cups of flour, 2 1/2 hours of work. Decide the operation first: "left" means subtract, "in all" means add, "times the recipe" means multiply, "how many pieces" means divide.

Practice: word problems with mixed numbers

Type a mixed number with a space (2 1/3), a fraction with a slash (7/3), or a whole number. Simplify the fraction part. Do not type the units.
