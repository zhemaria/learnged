What does dividing mean?

›

Division is the opposite of multiplication: it splits a total into equal groups, or finds how many groups fit. In 24 ÷ 6 = 4, 24 is the dividend (the total), 6 is the divisor, 4 is the quotient (the answer), and anything left over is the remainder.

Long division, step by step

›
Repeat the cycle until there are no more digits to bring down
StepWhat you do
DivideHow many times does the divisor fit? Write that number in the quotient.
MultiplyMultiply the divisor by that number.
SubtractSubtract to see what is left.
Bring downBring down the next digit and start again.

Step by step

The remainder is always smaller than the divisor. If you subtract and get a number equal to or bigger than the divisor, the digit in the quotient is too small: make it bigger. And if the divisor does not fit, write 0 in the quotient.

3

The remainder

›

When a division does not come out even, what is left over is the remainder. Check it like this:

(quotient × divisor) + remainder = dividend
Example: 50 ÷ 7 = 7, remainder 1, because (7 × 7) + 1 = 49 + 1 = 50 ✓.

What do I do with the remainder?

›

Finding the remainder is half of the work. The other half is deciding what it means in the real situation. The same calculation, 26 ÷ 4 = 6, remainder 2, has three different answers:

The three possibilities
What the problem asks forWhat you doExample
what is left overgive the remainder26 rolls in 4 equal boxes: 2 are left over
enough room for everyoneround the quotient up26 people in cars for 4: they need 7 cars
full groups onlydrop the remainder$26 and $4 tickets: you can buy 6 tickets
