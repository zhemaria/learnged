Prime numbers and prime factorization

›

A prime has exactly two factors: 1 and itself. To write the prime factorization, divide by the smallest prime that works, again and again, until you reach 1. Then check by multiplying.

4

The least common multiple (LCM)

›

List the multiples of both numbers and take the first one they share. Or use primes: take every prime that shows up, each with its greater exponent. Shortcut: LCM = (first × second) ÷ GCF. The step-by-step tool is in section 3.

5

GCF or LCM?

›

GCF: sharing or cutting into equal groups, as large as possible, with nothing left over. LCM: things that repeat, and when they happen together again.
