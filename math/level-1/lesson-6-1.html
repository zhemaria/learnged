1Measuring with U.S. units

To measure something, we compare it with a unit: a fixed amount we all agree on. In the United States, people use the U.S. customary system (inches, pounds, gallons…). It is the system you see on rulers, scales, recipes, and road signs, and it appears on the GED® test.

In this lesson you will work with three kinds of measures:

  • Length: how long, how tall, or how far.
  • Weight: how heavy.
  • Capacity: how much liquid a container holds.
The key idea of this lesson

To compare, add, or subtract two measures, they must be in the same unit. That is why we learn to convert: to change a measure from one unit to another without changing its size.

Abbreviations

The abbreviations (in, ft, yd, mi) are the ones you will see on rulers, maps, and the GED® test. Do not mix up ft (foot) and in (inch): 6 ft is a tall person; 6 in is a short pencil.

Example: how many inches tall is a door that is 7 feet tall?

1Find the equivalence. 1 foot = 12 inches.

2Each foot is 12 inches. 7 feet are 7 groups of 12: 7 × 12 = 84.

✓Answer. The door is 84 inches tall.

Watch out! Two kinds of ounces

An ounce (oz) measures weight. A fluid ounce (fl oz) measures capacity. A label that says "12 fl oz" tells how much drink is in the can, not how heavy the can is.

4Convert: multiply or divide?

To convert, ask yourself one question: am I going to a smaller unit or to a bigger unit?

A way to remember

Small units are like small coins: you need more of them. 1 dollar is 4 quarters. In the same way, 1 foot is 12 inches. Going to a smaller unit, the number gets bigger.

Example: how many pounds are 80 ounces?

1Big or small? Ounces are small; pounds are big. You go to a bigger unit, so you divide.

2Divide by the equivalence. 1 lb = 16 oz, so 80 ÷ 16 = 5.

✓Check it. 5 × 16 = 80 ✓. 80 ounces are 5 pounds.

Sometimes the division is not exact. Then we write the answer with two units, as on a growth chart: 75 inches = 6 feet and 3 inches.

Example: write 75 inches in feet and inches

1Divide by 12. 75 ÷ 12 = 6, remainder 3 (because 6 × 12 = 72, and 75 − 72 = 3).

2Read the result. The quotient is the feet; the remainder is the inches left over.

✓Answer. 75 inches = 6 ft 3 in.

5Homework

This is independent practice. Plan for about one hour, with your notebook open.

  • The practice page. Open the practice page for this lesson and do each activity. The exercises change every time. Do at least 10 in each activity, and keep going until you get 8 out of 10 right on the first try. The score under each activity keeps count for you.
  • Paper and pencil. Measure five things at home with a ruler or a tape measure: a table, a door, a window, your bed, and your height. Write each measure in inches, and then convert it to feet and inches.
  • In your daily life. Look at the labels of three products in your kitchen: one in ounces, one in pounds, and one in fluid ounces or gallons. Convert each one to another unit (for example, a 32 oz bag to pounds).

📘Key words

unit
A fixed amount used to measure, like an inch or a pound.
length
How long, how tall, or how far something is.
weight
How heavy something is.
capacity
How much liquid a container holds.
equivalence
Two measures that are the same size, like 1 ft = 12 in.
convert
To change a measure to another unit without changing its size.
