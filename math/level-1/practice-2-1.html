What does multiplying mean?

›

Multiplying is adding the same number several times, in a shorter way. Four packs of 6 bottles are 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 = 24, so 4 × 6 = 24. The numbers you multiply are the factors, and the answer is the product.

Times tables and properties

›

It helps to know the tables up to 12 × 12, like a carpenter knows the measurements. Press a square: its row and its column light up, and the "twin" square, with the factors switched, turns yellow. It has the same product.

Properties that make life easier
PropertyWhat it saysExample
Commutativethe order of the factors does not change the product7 × 8 = 8 × 7 = 56
Associativewith three factors, you can group them in any way(2 × 5) × 3 = 2 × (5 × 3) = 30
Distributiveyou can split a factor into easier parts25 × 4 = (20 × 4) + (5 × 4) = 100
Zero and onetimes 0 is 0; times 1 stays the same8 × 0 = 0 · 8 × 1 = 8

Multiplying by two digits

›

With two digits, find two partial products and add them. The second partial product, for the tens, moves one place to the left. That is why many people write a 0 that "holds the place."

Checking with an estimate

›

To see if a product is reasonable, round the factors and multiply the round numbers, which is easy. An estimate does not give the exact answer, but it is the alarm that catches a missing zero or a partial product in the wrong place.
