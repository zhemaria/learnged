Translate words into symbols

›

An unknown is a number we do not know yet. We write it with a box □ or a letter like x. Algebra writes ideas with symbols: "a number plus five" is x + 5.

Translation table
In wordsIn algebra
a number plus 7 · a number increased by 7 · 7 more than a numberx + 7
a number minus 5 · a number decreased by 5 · 5 less than a numberx − 5
twice a number2x
three times a number3x
a number divided by 4 · half of a number (divided by 2)x ÷ 4 · x ÷ 2
a number is equal to 12x = 12
Twice is written together: 2x, with no sign. A number written next to a letter means multiply. And "5 less than a number" is x − 5, not 5 − x: the number comes first.

The equation as a scale

›

An equation says that what is on one side of the = sign has the same value as what is on the other side, like a balanced scale. To solve it is to find the number in the box that keeps the scale level.

Solve with the inverse operation

›

The sure method: get the unknown alone with the inverse operation of what is with it. Subtraction undoes addition, and division undoes multiplication (and the other way around). And the rule of the scale: whatever you do to one side, do to the other side too.

The inverse operation for each case
EquationThe x is…Do this to both sides
x + 3 = 8added to 3subtract 3: x = 5
x − 4 = 10minus 4add 4: x = 14
4x = 20multiplied by 4divide by 4: x = 5
x ÷ 3 = 6divided by 3multiply by 3: x = 18

4

Check the solution

›

To know if an answer is right, substitute: put the number in place of x and calculate. If both sides are equal, the solution is right. In x + 6 = 15 with x = 9: 9 + 6 = 15 ✓.
