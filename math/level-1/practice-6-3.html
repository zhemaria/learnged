Lesson 6.3 · Applied measurement problems

Four practice activities on one page. The exercises change every time. If you make a mistake, you get a hint and another try.

Same unit first

›

Convert first, then calculate. If a division has a remainder, ask what it means: a piece that is too short does not count.

Equivalences
1 ft = 12 in1 yd = 3 ft = 36 in1 gal = 128 fl oz

Practice: solve the problem

Time and pay

Add and subtract with two units

›
Regroup at the size of the unit, not at 10: 12 inches = 1 foot, 16 ounces = 1 pound, 60 minutes = 1 hour, 4 quarts = 1 gallon.

Distance, speed, and time

›

Distance = speed × time. To find the time, divide: time = distance ÷ speed.
