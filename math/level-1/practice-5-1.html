Lesson 5.1 · Number and shape patterns

Three practice activities on one page. The exercises change every time. If you make a mistake, you get a hint and another try.

1

Number patterns

›

A pattern is a list of numbers that follows a rule. Each number is a term. To find the rule, look at what changes from one term to the next. If the numbers grow slowly and evenly, the rule is usually to add. If they grow very fast (they double or triple), the rule is usually to multiply.

Build your own pattern

Move the sliders and change the rule. Notice how adding makes the numbers grow evenly, and multiplying makes them grow very fast.

Practice: what comes next, or what is missing?

Shape patterns

›

Shapes can follow rules too. The trick: count something (squares, dots, sticks) and write the numbers. The shape pattern becomes a number pattern, and you already know how to find its rule.

Growing shapes

Practice: how many will it have?

Input-output tables

›

The input is the number you put into the "machine." The output is the number that comes out after the machine uses its rule. The rule connects each input with its own output: do not compare one row with the next one.

The rule machine

The machine has a hidden rule. Type an input and see what comes out. Try several inputs, then guess the rule.

Practice: what is the rule?
