From fraction to decimal

›

The fraction bar means "divided by". Divide the numerator by the denominator. If the numerator is smaller, write a point and zeros after it, and keep dividing.

Divide the fraction

Practice: which decimal is it?

🧮 Check it on the calculator

Type the numerator, ÷, the denominator, and enter. Or write the fraction with n/d and press ⟷ to see it as a decimal.

2

From decimal to fraction

›

Read the decimal. The last place gives the denominator: tenths → 10, hundredths → 100, thousandths → 1,000. Then simplify: divide the top and the bottom by the same number.

From the decimal to the fraction

Practice: write it as a simplified fraction

Type the fraction with a slash, for example 3/5.

🧮 Check it on the calculator

Type the decimal, press 2nd and table (the green words say f◂▸d), and press enter. The calculator shows the simplified fraction.

Decimals in real life
