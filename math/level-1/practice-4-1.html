What is a fraction?

›

The denominator (bottom) tells how many equal parts the whole is cut into. The numerator (top) tells how many of those parts you take.

Equivalent fractions

›

Multiply the top and the bottom by the same number. Each piece is cut into smaller pieces: there are more pieces, but the shaded amount stays the same.

Cut every piece

Practice: which one is equivalent?

3

Simplify a fraction

›

Divide the top and the bottom by the same number until no number greater than 1 divides both. You can go step by step with small numbers, or all at once with the greatest number that divides both.

Simplify step by step

Practice: simplify

Type the fraction with a slash, for example 2/3.

Write the fraction with the n/d template (numerator, ▼, denominator, ►) and press enter. The calculator shows it in simplest form.
