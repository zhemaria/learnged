1What is area?

The area is the amount of surface inside a shape. You need the area to buy carpet for a floor, paint for a wall, or grass for a yard. Perimeter goes around; area covers.

We measure area by counting square units: squares that are 1 unit on each side. Count the squares in this rectangle:

There are 3 rows of 5 squares: 3 × 5 = 15 square units. That is why the area of a rectangle is found by multiplying.

The key idea of this lesson

Area is always in square units: square inches (sq in or in²), square feet (sq ft or ft²), square meters (m²). The little 2 means "square," because we multiplied two lengths.

Example: a bedroom is 12 ft long and 10 ft wide. What is its area?

1Use the formula. A = 12 × 10 = 120.

✓Answer with square units. The area is 120 square feet (120 ft²).

Example: a square tile is 9 inches on each side. What is its area?

1Side times side. A = 9 × 9 = 81.

✓Answer. The area is 81 square inches.

Watch out! Do not mix up area and perimeter

For the bedroom, the perimeter is 12 + 10 + 12 + 10 = 44 ft (the baseboard). The area is 12 × 10 = 120 ft² (the carpet). Add for perimeter; multiply for area.

3Area of triangles

Every triangle is exactly half of a rectangle with the same base and the same height:

So the area of a triangle is half of base times height: A = ½ × b × h

Watch out! The height goes straight up

The height is the distance from the base to the opposite corner, measured straight up (perpendicular to the base, with a right angle). It is not the slanted side.

Example: a triangle has a base of 10 ft and a height of 6 ft. What is its area?

1Base times height. 10 × 6 = 60 (that is the whole rectangle).

2Take half. 60 ÷ 2 = 30.

✓Answer. The area is 30 square feet.

🧮 Check it on the calculator

Type 10 × 6 ÷ 2 enter. The screen shows 30.

4Area in real life

Many real floors and yards are not simple rectangles. Split the shape into rectangles, find each area, and add.

Example: the area of the L-shaped floor

1Part A. 8 × 5 = 40 ft².

2Part B. It is 4 ft wide, and its height is 8 − 5 = 3 ft: 4 × 3 = 12 ft².

✓Add the parts. 40 + 12 = 52 square feet.

Example: carpet costs $3 per square foot. How much does it cost to carpet a room 12 ft by 10 ft?

1Area. 12 × 10 = 120 ft².

✓Multiply by the price. 120 × $3 = $360.

5Homework

This is independent practice. Plan for about one hour, with your notebook open.

  • The practice page. Open the practice page for this lesson and do each activity. The exercises change every time. Do at least 10 in each activity, and keep going until you get 8 out of 10 right on the first try. The score under each activity keeps count for you.
  • Paper and pencil. Draw a rectangle on graph paper and count its squares. Then check with length × width. Draw a triangle with the same base and height and show that it is half of the rectangle.
  • In your daily life. Measure a room in your home. Find its area, and find how much it would cost to cover it with carpet at $3 per square foot. Find its perimeter too, for the baseboard.

📘Key words

area
The amount of surface inside a shape, measured in square units.
square unit
A square that is 1 unit on each side, like 1 ft² or 1 m².
base
The side of a triangle (or rectangle) we measure along the bottom.
height
The distance from the base to the opposite corner, measured straight up, at a right angle.
composite shape
A shape made of simpler shapes, like an L made of two rectangles.
