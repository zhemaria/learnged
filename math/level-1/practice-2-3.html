Keywords: multiply or divide?

›

We multiply when there are several equal groups and we want the total. We divide when there is a total and we want to split it into equal groups, or find how many groups fit.

Words that point to multiplying or dividing
✖️ Point to multiplying➗ Point to dividing
each … how many in all?split equally, share equally
the total of … equal groupshow much for each person? for each group?
times, twice, double, triplehow many groups?
per (with the number of groups given)half, one third, divided by
"Each" and "per" show up in both kinds of problems. Picture the situation: are equal groups coming together (multiply), or is a total being split (divide)?
