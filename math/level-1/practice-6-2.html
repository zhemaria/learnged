Convert metric units

›
Metric equivalences
LengthMassCapacity
1 km = 1,000 m1 kg = 1,000 g1 L = 1,000 mL
1 m = 100 cm = 1,000 mm1 g = 1,000 mg
1 cm = 10 mm
To a smaller unit: multiply (the point moves right). To a bigger unit: divide (the point moves left). Move one place for each zero: 1,000 has 3 zeros.

Practice: convert

Type only the number. You can use a decimal point, like 3.5.

Elapsed time

›

Count up in jumps: from the start time to the next full hour, then whole hours, then the minutes left. Example: 9:45 → 10:00 (15 min) → 1:00 (3 h) → 1:20 (20 min). Total: 3 h 35 min.

Practice: how much time passes?

Read a thermometer

›

First find how much each small mark is worth. On these thermometers the numbers go up by 10, with 5 spaces between them: each mark is 10 ÷ 5 = 2 degrees.
