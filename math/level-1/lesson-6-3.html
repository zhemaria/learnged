1Problems with several steps

In real life, measurement problems rarely take a single step. A recipe has to be doubled, a board has to be cut, a shift has a break that is not paid. In this lesson you will put together everything from Topic 6 and solve problems with two or more steps.

The 4 steps for measurement problems

1What do they ask, and in what unit? Underline the question and the unit of the answer.

2Put everything in the same unit. Convert before you calculate.

3Calculate, one step at a time.

4Check: does the answer make sense, and does it have the unit they asked for?

2First, the same unit

The most common trap in measurement problems is to calculate with two different units. Convert first.

Example: a board is 8 feet long. How many 18-inch pieces can you cut from it?

1Same unit. 8 feet = 8 × 12 = 96 inches.

2Divide. 96 ÷ 18 = 5, remainder 6.

✓Interpret the remainder. The 6 inches left over are not enough for another piece. You can cut 5 pieces.

Example: a recipe uses 2 cups of milk. You will make it 6 times. How many quarts of milk do you need?

1Total in cups. 6 × 2 = 12 cups.

2Convert to quarts. 1 quart = 4 cups, so 12 ÷ 4 = 3.

✓Answer. You need 3 quarts of milk.

Watch out! The remainder

When you cut or fill things, a remainder usually means "not enough for one more." But if you must buy enough, a remainder means you need one more: to carry 50 people in vans of 12, you need 5 vans, not 4.

3Time, money, and speed

Many everyday problems mix time with money or distance: how much you earn in a shift, or how long a trip takes.

Example: Ana works from 8:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Her 30-minute lunch is not paid. She earns $16 per hour. How much does she earn that day?

1Elapsed time. 8:30 → 9:00 is 30 min, 9:00 → 4:00 is 7 h: in total 7 h 30 min.

2Take away the break. 7 h 30 min − 30 min = 7 paid hours.

3Multiply by the pay per hour. 7 × $16 = $112.

✓Answer. Ana earns $112 that day.

A speed like 45 miles per hour is a rate: it says how many miles for each hour. Distance = speed × time.

Example: a bus travels 45 miles each hour. How long does it take to travel 180 miles?

1Equal groups of 45 miles. How many groups of 45 fit in 180? Divide: 180 ÷ 45 = 4.

✓Check and answer. 4 × 45 = 180 ✓. The trip takes 4 hours.

4Add and subtract with two units

Sometimes you add or subtract measures written with two units, like 2 ft 9 in. Add each unit by itself, and then regroup, as you carry in addition. The difference: you regroup at 12 inches, 16 ounces, or 60 minutes, not at 10.

Example: 2 ft 9 in + 1 ft 8 in

1Add each unit. 2 + 1 = 3 ft and 9 + 8 = 17 in: 3 ft 17 in.

2Regroup. 17 in = 1 ft 5 in (because 12 in = 1 ft).

✓Answer. 3 ft + 1 ft 5 in = 4 ft 5 in.

Example: 5 h 15 min − 2 h 40 min

1Not enough minutes. 15 min − 40 min cannot be done. Borrow 1 hour = 60 minutes: 5 h 15 min = 4 h 75 min.

2Subtract each unit. 4 − 2 = 2 h and 75 − 40 = 35 min.

✓Answer. 2 h 35 min.

The most common mistake

Regrouping at 10, as with whole numbers. 17 inches is not "1 ft 7 in": a foot has 12 inches, so 17 in = 1 ft 5 in.

5Homework

This is independent practice. Plan for about one hour, with your notebook open.

  • The practice page. Open the practice page for this lesson and do each activity. The exercises change every time. Do at least 10 in each activity, and keep going until you get 8 out of 10 right on the first try. The score under each activity keeps count for you.
  • Paper and pencil. Write two measurement problems from your own life with two steps each: one about a recipe or shopping, and one about your work schedule or a trip. Solve them with the 4 steps.
  • In your daily life. Next time you work or travel, write the start and end times and find the total time. If you are paid by the hour, find your pay for that day.

📘Key words

multistep problem
A problem that needs two or more operations to solve.
remainder
What is left over after a division. In real problems you must decide what it means.
rate
A comparison of two different units, like miles per hour or dollars per hour.
speed
The distance traveled in each unit of time. Distance = speed × time.
regroup
To change 12 inches into 1 foot (or 16 ounces into 1 pound, or 60 minutes into 1 hour) when you add or subtract measures.
