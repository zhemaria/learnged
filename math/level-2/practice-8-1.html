The mean

›

The mean, or average, is the sum of all the values divided by how many there are. It is what each one would get if everything were shared out equally.

3

The median

›

First put the data in order. With an odd number of values, the median is the one in the middle; with an even number, it is the average of the two in the middle.

Common mistake: taking the middle value of the list without putting it in order first.

The mode and the range

›

The mode is the value that repeats the most. There can be one, two (bimodal), or none. The range is greatest − least: it measures how spread out the data is.

The weighted mean

›
weighted mean = Σ(value × weight) ÷ Σ(weights)
