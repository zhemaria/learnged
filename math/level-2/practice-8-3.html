What simple probability is

›
P(A) = favorable outcomes ÷ total outcomes

The sample space is the list of all possible outcomes. Choose an experiment and an event, and count the favorable outcomes.

Practice: impossible, possible, or certain?

Fraction, decimal, and percent

›

The same probability can be written in three ways: 1/2 = 0.5 = 50%. Every probability is from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain).

Practice: the three forms

3

Typical probability problems

›

Count the total outcomes (all the marbles, all the cards, all the faces) and the favorable ones. The probability of not getting something is 1 minus the probability of getting it.

A standard deck has 52 cards: 4 suits (hearts and diamonds are red; clubs and spades are black) of 13 cards each (A, 2–10, J, Q, K).

Practice: find the probability

Write it as a fraction (3/8), a decimal (0.375), or a percent (37.5%).

Experiment and theory

›

The theoretical probability of each face of a die is 1/6 ≈ 16.7%. Roll the die and watch the experimental probability of each face.
