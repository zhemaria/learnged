A percent is a ratio out of 100

›

Percent means “for every hundred.” A percent is a ratio whose second term is always 100: 25% is 25 parts out of every 100, which you can also write 25/100 or 0.25.

3

The percent triangle

›

Every percent problem joins three pieces in one formula: Part = Percent × Whole, with the percent as a decimal. The triangle helps you remember which operation to use: cover what is missing and read what is left. Side by side, multiply; one above the other, divide.

Cover what you are missing

Which number is the whole? It is the reference amount, the one after the word “of”: “15% of $80,” “18 of 25.”

5

The percent proportion

›

Because a percent is a ratio out of 100, any problem can be set up as a direct proportion (Lesson 4.2). Put in the three values you know, put x where the missing one goes, and solve with cross products.

partwhole = %100

Set up the proportion

Which method is better? The formula is faster with a calculator; the proportion helps when you do not know which operation to use. Use one to check the other.
