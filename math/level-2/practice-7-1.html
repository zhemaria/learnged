Basic area formulas

Area formulas (area is always in square units)
FigureFormulaIn words
RectangleA = b × hbase times height
SquareA = s²side squared
ParallelogramA = b × hbase times height (the height, not the slanted side)
TriangleA = (b × h) ÷ 2base times height, divided by two
TrapezoidA = ((B + b) × h) ÷ 2sum of the bases times the height, divided by two

What a composite figure is

›

A composite figure is made of two or more simple figures: an L-shaped apartment, a yard with a corner cut off, a house with its roof. You measure it with one simple idea: divide and conquer. There are two ways:

The two strategies
StrategyWhat you doBest when…
Additionsplit it into pieces and add their areasthe figure splits easily into pieces
Subtractionfind the whole figure and subtract what is missingthe figure is a big one with a piece taken out

The addition strategy

The cost of materials

›

From area to budget: find the area and multiply by the price of each square unit. If the material comes in cans or rolls that cover a fixed amount, divide and round up: the store does not sell half a gallon of paint.
