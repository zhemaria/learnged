Direct or inverse

›

Let's build houses. Move the sliders and watch what grows and what shrinks.

Houses and cement: they go up together ↑↑

Workers and days: one goes up, the other goes down ↑↓

The two kinds of proportion
DirectInverse
If one quantity grows…the other growsthe other shrinks
If one doubles…the other doublesthe other is cut in half
What stays the samethe quotient (the ratio)the product
The test question: “If one quantity grows, what does the other do?” If it grows too, it is direct; if it shrinks, it is inverse.

Solving inverse proportions

›

In an inverse proportion, what stays the same is the product. Multiply the known values, then divide by the new quantity.

4 workers × 9 days = 36  →  6 workers × x days = 36  →  x = 6

Practice: inverse proportions

Type only the number. It may have decimals, like 1.25; for money you may use the $ sign.

3

Mixed applications

›

Problems do not say what kind they are. First ask the key question, then choose the method: constant ratio (direct) or constant product (inverse). Last, check that the answer makes sense.
