PEMDAS with fractions

›

Fractions do not change the order. To add or subtract, find a common denominator; to multiply, multiply straight across; to divide, multiply by the reciprocal. And the parentheses in (a/b)2 raise the whole fraction.

Practice: fractions with PEMDAS

Type an integer or a fraction like −7/2, in simplest form. If it is negative, start with the minus sign.
