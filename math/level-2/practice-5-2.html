Estimate before you calculate

›

A good habit is to estimate first whether the change is large or small compared with the starting value. That way you spot silly answers right away.

Benchmarks: 10% moves the decimal point one place to the left; 50% is half; 100% means the amount doubled.

Percent change

Percent change tells how much a quantity grew or shrank, as a percent of the starting value.

Absolute change and relative change

›

The absolute change tells how many units something changed; the relative change (the percent) tells how big that change is compared with the starting point.

The same increase on different prices

4

The change factor

›

If you know the percent and want the new value, you can find the change and add or subtract it, or multiply right away by the change factor: (1 + r) to increase, (1 − r) to decrease.
