Adding on the number line

›

Adding is moving on the number line: start at the first number and move. To add a positive number, move to the right; to add a negative number, move to the left.

The jump

Practice: where do you land?

Two-color counters

›

Each yellow counter (+) is worth +1, and each red counter (−) is worth −1. One yellow and one red make a zero pair, because +1 + (−1) = 0. Take away the zero pairs: what is left is the answer.

Make the zero pairs

Want to understand it with money? Open the complement What I have and what I owe (opens in a new tab): coins are what you have, and debts are what you owe.

Practice: what is left?

The addition rule

›

The number line and the counters lead to a rule that lets you add without drawing:

The rule for adding integers
CaseThe absolute valuesSign of the answer
Same signadd themthe sign they share
Different signssubtract the smaller from the largerthe sign of the number with the larger absolute value
The most common mistake: with different signs, do not add the absolute values. −13 + 8 is not −21: it is −5.

Practice: first the sign, then the value

Step 1. What is the sign of the answer?

If the answer is 0, you do not need to choose a sign.

4

Subtracting is adding the opposite

›

Every subtraction can become an addition: subtracting a number is adding its opposite. Then use the addition rule. There are no new rules to memorize.

a − b = a + (−b)

Rewrite and solve

Two minus signs in a row become a plus sign: "− (−9)" is "+ 9". Taking away a debt is like receiving money.

Practice: how do you rewrite it?

Practice: subtract

Type an integer. If it is negative, start with the minus sign: −7 or -7.

5

Real-life problems

›

The key is to give each amount the right sign: what goes up, is gained, or is deposited is positive; what goes down, is lost, or is taken out is negative.

Words and signs
Positive (+)Negative (−)
rises, goes up, above zerodrops, goes down, below zero
deposit, gain, incomewithdrawal, loss, debt, overdraft
above sea level, moves forwardbelow sea level, moves back
