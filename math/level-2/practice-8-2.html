What a circle graph is

›

A circle graph is a circle divided into sectors. Each sector is the percent of one category, and all the percents add up to 100%, because the whole circle is the total.

It is ideal to show how a whole is divided (a budget, a day, a population). To show change over time, a line graph is better.
