The area formula

›
A = π × r²

Cut the circle into slices and lay them alternately up and down: they form almost a parallelogram whose base is half the circumference (π × r) and whose height is the radius (r). The more slices, the closer it gets to a rectangle.

Cut the circle

Order of operations: square the radius first, then multiply by π. If r = 5, then r² = 25 and A = π × 25.

Practice: find the area

Use π ≈ 3.14 and round to two decimal places, or write the answer in terms of π, like 36π.

3

The radius from the area

›

Since the radius is squared, you need a square root at the end: first divide by π, then take the square root.

r² = A ÷ π     r = √(A ÷ π)
Example: a garden has an area of 78.5 ft². 78.5 ÷ 3.14 = 25, and √25 = 5 ft.

Practice: from the area to the radius

Type only the number, for example 5.

Figures with semicircles
