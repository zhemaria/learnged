Base and exponent

›

A power is a repeated multiplication. The base is the number that repeats, and the exponent tells how many times it is a factor: 53 = 5 × 5 × 5 = 125.

Build the power

The most common mistake: the exponent does not multiply the base. 53 is not 5 × 3 = 15; it is 5 × 5 × 5 = 125.

Special cases of the exponent

›

With the exponent 1, the answer is the base itself: 91 = 9. With the exponent 0, the answer is always 1: 80 = 1. And with a negative base in parentheses, the sign is multiplied too: positive if the exponent is even, negative if it is odd.

The perfect squares from 1 to 225

›

A perfect square is the result of squaring a whole number. Learn the first fifteen by heart: they are the tool for square roots in the next lesson.

Flash cards

Each card shows a power; press it to see the value. Try to say the value before you flip the card.

Strategy: learn them in groups of five: 1 to 25, 36 to 100, and 121 to 225.
