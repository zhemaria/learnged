The three forms of a negative fraction

›

A fraction is a division, so its sign follows the sign rule. The minus sign can go in front, in the numerator, or in the denominator, and the value does not change. If the numerator and the denominator are both negative, the two signs cancel and the fraction is positive.

Move the signs

The usual way: write the minus sign in front of the fraction or in the numerator. In the denominator it is correct, but easy to misread.

Multiplying and dividing fractions with signs

›

Work in two separate steps: first the sign, with the sign rule, and then the value, with the absolute values. To divide, multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction.

Step by step

When you find the reciprocal, you flip the numbers, but the sign was already decided at the start with the sign rule: it does not get lost.

Practice: multiply or divide

Type a fraction with a slash, like −3/10, with the minus sign in front if it is negative. Give it in simplest form.

Adding and subtracting fractions with signs

›

To add or subtract you need a common denominator. With the same denominators, add or subtract the numerators with the integer rules from Topic 1. And subtracting a negative fraction is adding its opposite.

Expressions with an integer and a fraction

›

Treat an integer as a fraction with denominator 1 and bring it to the common denominator. For example, −2 = −8/4. You can leave the answer as an improper fraction (−11/4) or as a mixed number (−2 3/4).

Practice: integer and fraction

Type a fraction like −11/4 or a mixed number like −2 3/4 (with a space between the whole number and the fraction).
