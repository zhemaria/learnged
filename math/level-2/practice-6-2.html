First the table, then the formula

›

The total loss is the starting value minus the salvage value. It is split into equal parts among the years of useful life, and each year that amount is subtracted from the value of the year before. The value never drops below the salvage value.

Fill in the table

Type only numbers, without the $ sign if you like: 2,200 or 2200. If you make a mistake, you get a hint and another try; on the second try, the complete table appears.

3

The straight-line depreciation formulas

›
D = (V₀ − Vr) ÷ n     book value in year t = V₀ − D × t

The yearly rate of straight-line depreciation is 1 ÷ n: if an item lasts 4 years, each year it loses one fourth (25%) of the value being depreciated.

Depreciation calculator

Common mistake: dividing the starting value by the useful life without first subtracting the salvage value. With 3,000 ÷ 5 = 600, the machine would end up worth $0, not the $500 the problem says.

Practice: straight-line depreciation

Type only the number. If a rate is asked, write it as a percent (20 for 20%).

Combined money problems

›

In real life a loan with interest, a tax, and the loss of value of what you buy all come together. The strategy: solve each part on its own, then put the results together.

The three tools of Topic 6 so far
Part of the problemTool
A tax or a discountprice × (1 + rate) or price × (1 − rate)
A loan or savingsI = P × r × t; A = P + I
An item that loses valueD = (V₀ − Vr) ÷ n; V₀ − D × t
