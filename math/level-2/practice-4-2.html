What a proportion is

›

A proportion is an equation between two equal ratios: “a is to b as c is to d.” To find out whether two ratios form a proportion, simplify both or compare their cross products.

The constant-ratio test

›

Two quantities are in direct proportion when the quotient of each pair of values (second ÷ first) is always the same. If the quotient changes, it is not proportional, even if both quantities grow together. A fixed charge always breaks the proportion.

3

The cross-product rule

›

If two ratios are equal, their cross products are equal too. With that rule you solve for the missing term, wherever it is.

If ab = cd, then a × d = b × c

Step by step

Cross products work only with an equation between two ratios. They are not for adding or subtracting fractions, or for a single fraction.

4

Solving direct proportions

›

In a direct proportion, write the two ratios in the same order: if the flour of the first case is on top, the flour of the second case is on top too. Then use cross products.

4 cups12 cookies = x cups30 cookies  →  12x = 120  →  x = 10
