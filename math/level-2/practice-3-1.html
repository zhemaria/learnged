The four levels of priority

The four levels of PEMDAS
LevelWhat you do
1 · Pgrouping symbols: ( ), [ ], { }, from the inside out
2 · Eexponents and roots
3 · MDmultiplication and division, from left to right
4 · ASaddition and subtraction, from left to right
Six letters, four levels. M and D share a level, and so do A and S. In 20 ÷ 4 × 5, divide first because it is farther left.
