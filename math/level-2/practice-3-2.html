Powers and roots

›

Powers and roots share the second level of PEMDAS. And everything under the √ sign is grouped as if it were in parentheses: √(16 + 9) = √25 = 5, not √16 + √9 = 7.

Step by step

Practice: powers and roots

Type an integer. If it is negative, start with the minus sign: −7 or -7.

The fraction bar groups

›

The fraction bar groups the numerator and the denominator. Work out each one separately, and divide at the end. On one line: (numerator) ÷ (denominator).

Step by step

Practice: with a fraction bar

Type an integer. If it is negative, start with the minus sign: −7 or -7.

3

PEMDAS with negative numbers

›

With parentheses, the sign is part of the base: (−5)2 = 25. With no parentheses, the exponent applies only to the number, and the minus sign is applied at the end: −52 = −25.

With and without parentheses

Practice: with negative numbers

Type an integer. If it is negative, start with the minus sign: −7 or -7.

4

The order referee

›

Read each solution like a referee. Is every step the operation with the highest priority? If a step breaks the order, choose it. If every step is right, choose "None."

Practice: in which step is the mistake?
