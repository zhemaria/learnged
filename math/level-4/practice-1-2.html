The slope in motion

›

Drag point A or point B with the mouse or your finger, or tap any grid crossing to move the last point you used. With the keyboard, press Tab to reach a point and move it with the arrows. The triangle shows the run (Δx, across) and the rise (Δy, up or down) between the two points.

Challenge: build the line

Place A and B so that the line has this slope:

How the position of the points changes the slope
If you move the points like this……the slope does this
B higher than A, to the rightm is positive: the line goes up.
B lower than A, to the rightm is negative: the line goes down.
A and B at the same heightΔy = 0, so m = 0: the line is horizontal.
A and B in the same columnΔx = 0, and you cannot divide by 0: the slope is undefined.
The points move apart without changing the tiltThe slope does not change: it is the same all along the line.

2

The slope from two points

›
m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁)

The top is the rise (Δy); the bottom is the run (Δx). Subtract in the same order on top and on the bottom: the point you subtract first in one place is the point you subtract first in the other.

Practice: find the slope

Type a whole number or a fraction like −3/4. If the run is 0, type “undefined.”

The four types of slope

›

The line starts at (−3, 0). Change the rise and the run, or choose a type directly. Read lines from left to right.

The four types of slope; the row of the current type is highlighted
TypeWhat does the line look like?Value of m
PositiveIt goes up from left to right.m > 0
NegativeIt goes down from left to right.m < 0
ZeroHorizontal (flat).m = 0
UndefinedVertical.m does not exist (run = 0)
Which is steeper? The one with the larger absolute value of the slope, |m|. A line with m = −5 is steeper than one with m = 3, even though one goes down and the other goes up.

4

The slope as a rate of change

›

Outside the plane, the slope is a rate of change: how much one quantity changes for each unit of the other, in “units of y per unit of x.” Choose a situation and any two rows of the table: because the graph is a straight line, the rate always comes out the same.

The sign has a meaning too. A positive rate means the quantity grows; a negative one, that it shrinks. A tank that drains at −12 gallons per minute loses 12 gallons every minute: say it in words, not only with the sign.
