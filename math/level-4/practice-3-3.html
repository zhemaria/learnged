Trinomials with a = 1: two numbers

›

To factor x² + bx + c, look for two numbers whose product is c and whose sum is b. The table lists every pair of whole numbers with product c; look at the sum column and find b.

Trinomials with a ≠ 1: the ac method

›

Both roads start the same way: find two numbers whose product is a · c and whose sum is b. They also end the same way. Choose a road and compare.

Multiplying and dividing by the same number does not change the trinomial. That is why the divisor must disappear when you simplify: if it does not, one of the two numbers is wrong.

Special cases and complete factoring

The order of work never changes: first the common factor, then the trinomial. Some trinomials are perfect squares, and some cannot be factored with whole numbers.
