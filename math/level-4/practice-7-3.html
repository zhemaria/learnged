The average rate of change

›

The average rate of change from x = a to x = b is the change in y over the change in x: the slope of the secant line (red) that joins the two points.

rate = (f(b) − f(a)) / (b − a)

Practice: find the rate

You can type a whole number, a decimal, or a fraction such as 7/2.
