The zero product property

›
If A · B = 0, then A = 0 or B = 0.

The property can only be used when the product equals zero. First decide whether you can use it yet; then go from the factors to the solutions.

Practice: can you use the property yet?

Practice: from the factors to the solutions

The key is the zero. If a product is 12, there are many possible pairs of numbers. If it is 0, there is no other way: one of the factors has to be zero. If the two factors are equal, there is only one solution.

3

Standard form first

›

Many equations do not come equal to zero. Step 1 is always to move every term to one side until you reach the standard form ax² + bx + c = 0.

Typical mistake: factoring without the zero. x² + 5x = 6 → x(x + 5) = 6 → x = 6 or x + 5 = 6? Check x = 6: 36 + 30 = 66, not 6. The method does not work because the product is not zero. The right way: x² + 5x − 6 = 0, (x + 6)(x − 1) = 0, so x = −6 or x = 1. Check: (−6)² + 5(−6) = 36 − 30 = 6. ✓
When a term moves to the other side, its sign changes
Term on the right sideOperation on both sidesHow it appears on the left
+ 6Subtract 6− 6
− 8Add 8+ 8
+ 5xSubtract 5x− 5x
− 2xAdd 2x+ 2x

Do not divide by x: in x² = 5x, dividing both sides by x loses the solution x = 0. Move everything to one side and factor.

Word problems: interpret the solution

›

Write the equation, solve it by factoring, and then decide which of the two solutions makes sense in the real situation.

Steps 2 to 4. Factor and find both solutions.
