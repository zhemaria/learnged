The five steps of substitution

›

1. Solve one equation for one variable. 2. Substitute that expression into the other. 3. Solve the equation with one variable. 4. Substitute that value to find the other variable. 5. Check in both equations.

Solve for the variable whose coefficient is 1 or −1, to avoid fractions, and use parentheses when you substitute.
