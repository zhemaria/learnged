Lesson 5.2 · Adding, subtracting, multiplying, and dividing rational expressions

Four practice activities on one page. The exercises change every time. If you make a mistake, you get a hint and another try.

Multiplying

›

Factor, cancel any common factor (a top one with a bottom one, even across the two fractions), and multiply what is left. Cancelling before multiplying saves a lot of work.

Practice: choose the simplified product

2

Dividing

›
a/b ÷ c/d = a/b · d/c

Dividing is multiplying by the reciprocal: keep the first fraction, change ÷ to ·, and flip the second. Then it is a multiplication.

Practice: choose the simplified quotient

3

Adding and subtracting with the same denominator

›

When the denominators are the same, combine the numerators and keep the denominator. In a subtraction, the minus sign goes to the whole second numerator.

Practice: add or subtract

↑ Back to contents
4

Adding and subtracting with different denominators

›

Find the least common denominator (LCD), rewrite each fraction with it, and combine the numerators: the same process as 1/2 + 1/3 = 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.

The LCD of numbers and powers of x: the LCM of the numbers and the highest power of x (for x and 2x², it is 2x²). The LCD of two different binomials is their product.

Practice: choose the result
