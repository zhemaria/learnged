What is a polynomial?

›

A polynomial is made of terms added or subtracted. Each term has a coefficient (the number) and a power of x with a whole-number exponent, 0 or more. Choose what to highlight, ask for another polynomial, or put it in standard form.

Each sign goes with the term after it: in 3x − 5 the second term is −5. When no number is written in front of x, the coefficient is 1: in x² it is 1 and in −x³ it is −1.

When is an expression not a polynomial?

Expressions that are and are not polynomials
ExpressionPolynomial?Reason
x²/2 + 0.5x − 3YesThe coefficients are fractions or decimals, but the exponents (2, 1, and 0) are whole numbers.
−8YesA monomial of degree 0.
4x−2 + 1NoThe exponent −2 is negative.
6x1/2 − xNoThe exponent 1/2 is a fraction.
5/x + 2Nox is in a denominator (5/x is 5x⁻¹).

Adding and subtracting polynomials

›

Golden rule: only like terms go together, the ones with the same power of x. It is like sorting laundry: shirts with shirts, pants with pants. Only the numbers are added or subtracted; the x part is copied.

The most common traps
#WrongRightWhy
1✗ 3x² + 5x² = 8x⁴✓ 3x² + 5x² = 8x²When you add, the exponent is copied.
2✗ 3x² + 2x = 5x³✓ 3x² + 2x (already simplified)x² and x are different families.
3✗ −(2x² − x + 4) = −2x² − x + 4✓ −(2x² − x + 4) = −2x² + x − 4The minus changes every sign.
4✗ 7 − 3x + 5x = 7 + 8x✓ 7 − 3x + 5x = 7 + 2xEach term keeps the sign on its left.
5✗ x² + 4x² = 4x²✓ x² + 4x² = 5x²x² has an invisible 1.

A monomial times a polynomial

›
a(b + c + d) = ab + ac + ad

The monomial visits every term of the parentheses, one by one. In each product there are two separate jobs: the numbers are multiplied (with the sign rule), and the exponents of x are added. A lone x has exponent 1, and a number alone has no x.

The most common traps
#WrongRightWhy
1✗ 5(x + 2) = 5x + 2✓ 5(x + 2) = 5x + 10The monomial multiplies every term.
2✗ −3x(x − 4) = −3x² − 12x✓ −3x(x − 4) = −3x² + 12xSame signs give positive.
3✗ x² · x³ = x⁶✓ x² · x³ = x⁵The exponents are added.
4✗ 4x · 3x = 7x²✓ 4x · 3x = 12x²The numbers are multiplied.
5✗ 3x · 5 = 15✓ 3x · 5 = 15xThe x does not disappear.

4

Evaluating a polynomial

›

To evaluate, put the number in place of x, always in parentheses, and follow the order of operations: powers, then multiplications, then additions and subtractions.

Parentheses for negative numbers. (−3)² = 9, but −3² = −9. When you put a negative number in place of x, write it in parentheses so the exponent reaches the sign too.
