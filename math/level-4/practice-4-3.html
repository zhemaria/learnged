The discriminant: how many solutions?

›

The discriminant is the part inside the square root of the quadratic formula: b² − 4ac. Its sign tells how many real solutions the equation has before you solve it.

How the sign of the discriminant tells the number of real solutions
Discriminant b² − 4acReal solutionsThe parabola and the x-axis
Greater than 02 different solutionsCrosses it at 2 points
Equal to 01 solution (a double one)Touches it at 1 point
Less than 0No real solutionDoes not touch it

What does “no real solution” mean? No real number solves the equation, so the parabola never crosses the x-axis. The answer lives in a larger set of numbers (the complex numbers) studied in later courses; it is not part of this level or of the GED test.

Discriminant calculator. Type the coefficients of ax² + bx + c = 0.

Before solving, ask: how many solutions do I expect? If you get a different number at the end, there is a sign mistake somewhere.

2

Solving with the quadratic formula

›
x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a

Type the coefficients and reveal one step at a time. Try to predict each step before you show it.

The most common mistake: forgetting that −b changes the sign of b. If b = −4, then −b = +4, and b² = (−4)² = 16, never −16.

3

The path of a ball

›
h(t) = −16t² + v₀t + h₀

v₀ is the starting speed (feet per second) and h₀ is the height it is thrown from (feet). The −16 comes from gravity and is always the same in feet.

Interpret the situation. Solving h(t) = 0 gives two solutions, but time cannot be negative. The valid answer is the positive one.

Three methods: which one is best?

›

Factoring, completing the square, and the formula reach the same solutions, but they do not take the same work.

The three methods and when each one is best
MethodWhen is it best?
FactoringWhen the trinomial factors easily with whole numbers.
Completing the squareWhen a = 1 and b is even.
Quadratic formulaAlways works; best when it does not factor or the roots are not whole numbers.
Ask three questions, in this order. Does the trinomial factor easily with whole numbers? Then factor. If not, is a = 1 and b even? Then complete the square. In any other case, use the formula, which always works.
