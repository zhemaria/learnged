Social Studies practice test

35 questions · 70 minutes · one attempt · calculator

Before you start

  • 35 questions, chosen at random from the GED-style questions of Topics 3 to 8, with the test’s mix: 18 civics and government, 7 U.S. history, 5 economics, and 5 geography. Each attempt is different.
  • 70 minutes, no break. The clock starts when you press Start and does not stop. When time runs out, the test is submitted by itself.
  • Allowed: the on-screen calculator (button at the top of the page). Nothing else: no notes, no lessons, no phone.
  • Pace: at minute 35, be at question 17 or 18. Keep the last ten minutes for marked questions.
  • Never leave a question blank. There is no penalty for a wrong answer.
  • This practice test uses multiple-choice questions. Practice the other item types (drag and drop, drop-down, fill in the blank, hot spot) on the practice page of Lesson 8.3. It is not scored like the official test, which reports a scaled score.

Do it in real conditions: a quiet place, seventy minutes in a row, and let people at home know not to interrupt you. Before you look at your score, write down how you think you did.

How to review a mistake: do not just look at the right answer. Ask yourself why the one you chose looked right. There is almost always a reason, and that reason is the real mistake to fix.
