Science practice test

40 questions · 90 minutes · one attempt · calculator and formula sheet

Before you start

  • 40 questions, chosen at random from the GED-style questions of Topics 3 to 8, with the test’s mix: 16 life science, 16 physical science, and 8 Earth and space science. Each attempt is different.
  • 90 minutes, no break. The clock starts when you press Start and does not stop. When time runs out, the test is submitted by itself.
  • Allowed: the on-screen calculator and the formula sheet (buttons at the top of the page). Nothing else: no notes, no lessons, no phone.
  • Pace: by minute 30, try to be past question 13; by minute 60, past question 27. Mark the hard ones and come back.
  • Never leave a question blank. There is no penalty for a wrong answer.
  • This practice test uses multiple-choice questions. Practice the other item types (drag and drop, drop-down, fill in the blank, hot spot) on the practice page of Lesson 8.3. It is not scored like the official test, which reports a scaled score.

Do it in real conditions: a quiet place, ninety minutes in a row, and let people at home know not to interrupt you.
