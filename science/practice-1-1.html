Put the report in order

›

Read each paragraph and choose its stage in the list under it. One paragraph is not part of the investigation: choose “Does not belong in the report.” If something is not right, the paragraphs marked in yellow get a hint.

2

Observation or interpretation?

›

Does the sentence report what was seen or measured, or does it explain it? Look at the verbs: “because,” “shows that,” and “causes” usually point to an interpretation.

↑ Back to contents
3

Can it be tested?

›

A statement can be tested if some result could prove it wrong. That does not mean it is true: it means science can decide about it.

↑ Back to contents
4

Make it testable

›

Each statement below cannot be tested. Choose the version that science could test.

↑ Back to contents
5

Hypothesis, theory, or law?

›

A hypothesis is a prediction to test; a theory explains; a law describes a pattern that always holds.
